Creator questions

Built for live chaos, kept simple.

Is this made specifically for TikTok LIVE?

Yes. The product direction is focused on TikTok LIVE creators who want an interactive battle overlay for viewer-driven engagement.

Can I use it with OBS or TikTok Studio?

Yes. In OBS, add the overlay as a browser source. In TikTok Studio, open the overlay in a browser window and add it as a window capture.

Do viewers need to install anything?

No. The intended experience is driven by live stream actions such as chat, likes, follows, and gifts. Viewers interact from the stream.
