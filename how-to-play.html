Current player command manual

Engage once. Fight on screen.

IntoTheBattle turns TikTok LIVE engagement into a playable arena. Viewers auto-join from normal engagement, then use short chat commands to attack players, pressure The Horde, fight bosses, shield, heal, team up, and customize their fighter card.

Quick start

Any stream engagement enters a viewer into the arena. Attack by mentioning a target; effect and utility words work without a prefix. Existing ! and / prefixes remain supported.

comment / like

Enter the arena

Any comment, like, follow, share, repost, or gift creates your fighter or rallies you back into the live battle. !join is optional.

@name

Hit a player

Attacks a named fighter. You can also type !hit, !strike, or !fight.

shield

Block damage

Blocks regular attacks for 10 seconds. Gifts can break it, then shield has a 45-second cooldown.

heal @name

Recover HP

Heals the named fighter. Without a target, it heals you.

Pick a target

Attack actions always land on a player, The Horde, or the boss. That keeps animations and effects attached to something visible.

Player

Use @name, attack @name, or an effect like laser @name to hit a specific player. The target mention can appear anywhere in the comment.

The Horde

Use @horde or fire @horde. The overlay shows the hit against The Horde while the server resolves a horde fighter behind it.

Boss wave

Use @boss or orbital @boss while a boss wave is active to damage the shared boss health bar.

No mention

Regular comments without a target enter or rally the viewer and build engagement, but do not damage another fighter.

Entry and identity

Engage once, rally, and style your fighter.
comment / like

Enter automatically

Any comment or like creates your fighter if you are new, or rallies your existing fighter back into view.

!join

Manual entry

Optional. Also accepts !spawn, !play, start, enter, and ready.

follow / share

Social entry

Follows, shares, and reposts enter you and can trigger rally rewards for the room.

gift

Power entry

Gifts enter you, boost your fighter, launch stronger visual attacks, and break active shields.

!emoji <emoji>

Change icon

Sets the icon on your fighter card. Direct shorthand also works, such as !⚔.

!🔥

Icon shorthand

Typing an emoji directly after ! or / changes your fighter icon.

Attack targets

Simple commands for player, horde, and boss damage.
@name

Basic player hit

The recommended beginner attack. You can also use attack @name, hit @name, strike @name, or fight @name.

@horde

Hit The Horde

Targets the crowd-side reserve fighters and displays the effect on The Horde.

@boss

Hit the boss

Damages the active boss wave. Effects like laser @boss also work while a boss wave is active.

laser @name

Named effect attack

Uses a specific visual effect instead of the basic attack label.

!overdrive

Power-up command

Contextual. During Arena Power-Up, !overdrive can trigger the stronger barrage rotation.

Defense and support

Stay alive and keep allies in the fight.
shield

Guard yourself

Raises a 10-second shield that blocks regular player and boss attack damage.

!guard

Shield alias

Works like shield. defend and protect also work.

!block

Block alias

Works like shield. A shield cannot be refreshed while already active.

gift

Break shields

Gifts can break an active shield, clear it immediately, and start the shield cooldown.

45s cooldown

Shield lockout

After a shield ends or breaks, that player cannot add another shield for 45 seconds.

heal @name

Heal a fighter

Restores HP for the named fighter. Type heal alone to heal yourself. hp and health also work.

Teams and help

Coordinate with the room.
!team red

Join a team

Sets your team name and color. Any clean team name can be used. !squad red also works.

!team leave

Leave team

Clears your current team. !team clear, !team none, and !team solo also work.

commands

Show command list

Requests the current command reminder on the battle overlay. !comments also works.

!help

Help alias

Works like commands. Other help aliases include command, cmd, and howto.

Engagement triggers

Normal TikTok LIVE actions enter viewers into the arena and feed the fight. These are not typed commands.

Comments

Enter or rally the viewer. Add @name, @horde, or @boss when you want the comment to attack.

Likes

Enter the viewer, emit heart energy, and help keep the overlay active.

Gifts

Enter the viewer, create stronger visual attacks, break active shields, and add temporary message impact boosts.

Follows

Enter or rally a fighter and add a defensive shield moment when the shield cooldown allows it.

Shares

Enter the viewer and support squad-wide rally rewards during the battle.

Reposts

Enter the viewer, add social boost pressure, and help the room survive bigger moments.

Battle rules

The game keeps live chat readable by routing attacks into clear visible outcomes.

Top cards vs Horde

The top visible fighters stay on cards. The rest of the room forms The Horde, so a busy chat can still affect the battle.

Effects need targets

Attack animations attach to a player, The Horde, or the boss. Loose effects are not fired into empty space.

Respawns keep it moving

Knocked-out fighters can return, and the next comment, like, follow, share, repost, or gift can rally viewers back into the arena.

Shields have counters

Shield blocks regular attack damage for 10 seconds, but gifts can break it. After it ends or breaks, the player waits 45 seconds before shielding again.
