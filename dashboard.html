Creator responsibility

Before You Use IntoTheBattle

IntoTheBattle is the future of engagement games on TikTok. Please use it responsibly while we continue building the app.

  1. Do not use copyrighted sounds. Sounds must be self-made, properly licensed, or royalty free.
  2. Do not upload anyone else's video content. The video uploader is a major privilege and must only be used with content you own or have explicit rights to use.
  3. Anyone found violating these rules may be permanently banned from the platform with no exceptions.

By continuing, you acknowledge these rules and understand that violations can permanently remove your access.
