## SEO Best Practices Applied

### Meta Descriptions
- ✅ **Length:** 155-160 characters (optimal for Google SERP display)
- ✅ **Keywords:** Primary keyword in first 60 characters
- ✅ **Call-to-Action:** Action-oriented language
- ✅ **Unique:** Each page has unique description

### Open Graph Tags
- ✅ `og:type` - website, SoftwareApplication
- ✅ `og:title` - optimized for social sharing
- ✅ `og:description` - compelling summary for Facebook/LinkedIn
- ✅ `og:url` - canonical URL for sharing
- ✅ `og:image` - consistent branding image
- ✅ `og:site_name` - IntoTheBattle branding

### Twitter Cards
- ✅ `twitter:card` - summary_large_image for visual pages
- ✅ `twitter:title` - optimized for 70 characters
- ✅ `twitter:description` - engaging tweet-length summary
- ✅ `twitter:image` - visual content for shares

### Structured Data (JSON-LD)
- ✅ **Home Page:** @graph with WebSite, SoftwareApplication, Organization
- ✅ **User Pages:** WebPage with isPartOf WebSite relationship
- ✅ **App Pages:** WebApplication with applicationCategory
- ✅ **Navigation:** All pages link to parent WebSite

### Robots & Canonicals
- ✅ **Public Pages:** index, follow (home, login, register, signup, paywall, password-reset)
- ✅ **Private Pages:** noindex, follow (dashboard, profile, stats, overlay, verify-email)
- ✅ **Canonical URLs:** Each page points to its primary URL
- ✅ **Consistency:** Single canonical source for each page

### Technical SEO
- ✅ **Charset:** UTF-8 on all pages
- ✅ **Viewport:** Responsive meta viewport set
- ✅ **Favicon:** browser tab icon + apple-touch-icon
- ✅ **Language:** html lang="en" attribute
- ✅ **Author:** meta author tag on all pages
